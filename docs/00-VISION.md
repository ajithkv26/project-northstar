# Vision

## Problem
Design leaders skim portfolios quickly and look for judgment. Most portfolios show visuals and process steps, not how the designer decides.

## Audience
Design Directors, Heads of Design, product leaders.

## Goals
- Demonstrate product thinking
- Demonstrate systems thinking
- Demonstrate UX maturity
- Demonstrate design craftsmanship
- Stay relevant for years

## Principles
Reduce complexity. Create clarity. Design for change. Prefer timeless solutions over trends. Think long-term. Prioritise accessibility. Use restraint instead of decoration. Every decision has a reason.
